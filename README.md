## The Idea

Most HR systems are built for large corporations, priced like them, and designed in English first. Small businesses end up managing people on spreadsheets and chat groups.

**Kadr** is built for the rest. A contracting company, a restaurant, and a clinic all manage people differently, so Kadr starts from an **industry template** and lets each company shape it to its own rules, instead of forcing everyone into one generic setup.

## Highlights

- **Arabic-first, fully bilingual** — native RTL layout with English alongside, built for the Egyptian and Saudi markets.
- **Industry templates** — pick your industry at sign-up and start with ready shifts, leave types, document types, job titles, and departments, then edit them to fit.
- **Multi-tenant by design** — every company's data is isolated from the others.
- **Roles and invitations** — invite your team and control what each person can see and do.
- **Branding** — each company sets its own name and logo across its workspace.
- **Shifts, branches and locations** — assign shifts and work locations to people, departments, or roles.
- **Leave management** — requests and approvals in one place.
- **Audit log** — a clear record of who changed what, and when.
- **Plans and subscriptions** — flexible pricing with a built-in trial.
- **Reports** — an MVP set of HR reports.
- **Installable and push-ready** — a PWA that works like an app on the phone, with in-app, email, and web push notifications.

## Roadmap

Payroll is the next phase, with the rules (insurance, funds, tax, pay cycle, proration) controlled by each company:

- [x] Core platform: onboarding, branding, templates, users and roles
- [x] Plans, subscriptions, and audit log
- [x] Shifts, locations, leave, and reports
- [ ] Salary structure and payroll runs
- [ ] Loans, adjustments, and payslips
- [ ] Bank and accounting export, final settlement
- [ ] Saudi compliance pack
- [ ] Marketing landing page
